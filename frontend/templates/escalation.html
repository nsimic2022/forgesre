Default escalation {% with %}{% set tip %}Used when an asset Escalation is empty (same 30s loop as before, not a ticket system). Each step is minutes after the incident opened and an email recipient: an address is mailed as written, a role (team, team-lead, engineer) mails the asset owner email, and a role with no owner email is recorded as no-recipient and not sent — ForgeSRE does not invent role@forgesre.local addresses. The 0 min step is the mail at open; without it, nothing is sent at open. Email is the only channel: there is no webhook, SMS, or chat step. Seeded Default warning (0 / 15 / 30 min) is what new rules attach unless you pick another policy here. An asset with any Escalation level filled does not also use this page. Edit changes escalation for incidents that open after Save. Remove moves its rules to Default warning. Acknowledge stops further steps. SMTP off means the row stays generated — that is the lab outbox. Scheduled reports are a different mail path.{% endset %}{% include "_info_tip.html" %}{% endwith %}
