Each step is minutes after the incident opened and an email recipient. Acknowledge stops further steps.

  • Email address on the step (e.g. 30 oncall@dc.local) — mail goes to that address.
  • Role on the step (team, team-lead, engineer) — mail goes to the asset owner email (demo: platform@forgesre.local on forge-demo-01).
  • Role step and no owner email — recorded in the outbox as no-recipient and not sent. ForgeSRE does not invent role@forgesre.local addresses.
  • The 0 min step is the mail sent when the incident opens. No 0 min step = no mail at open.
